CodeLog is a notebook kept in public: Python internals, systems, and the parts
of the craft that are hard to search for.

Posts are written either as Jupyter notebooks or as plain Markdown, then built
into a static site. The source for everything, site included, is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/abhijeetadarsh/blog).
